YOUN
YOUN Station Notes:
  • Line Fill with GAS: Reg. Flush, Switch to DIESEL when 100% Gravity.
  • Line Fill with DIESEL: Reg. Flush, Switch to GAS when Gravity AT 50 degree.
  • Full tank full: Volume to fill - 100 bbls line fill - 35 extra space = barrels add to both tickets.

VANP
VANP Station Notes:
  • To switch from SUC control to DIS, lower DIS 100 psi and wait 3 mins.
  • Down Stream valve has High Alarm @ 995 because the RELEAVE valve @ ALLE has WARNING @ 995 and High warning @ 1017.
  • @ VANP set point SUC @ 60 psi, DISC @ 1020 psi, and meter down stream of VANP set point @ 930 - 940 psi.
  • When DIS at control around 1020 psi, slowly let out the pressure by increasing DIS to 1030 psi.
  • Swing from ALLE to DELM: Change SUCT 200 psi, DISC 950 psi. Get to DISC control instead of SUCT.
  • Lost power at VANP: open V2331 & then V2341. Facility reset, Suction 120, and start the pump.
ALLE
ALLE Station Notes:
  • Relief Valve: Low 995 psi, H 1045 psi.
  • IF Going End of Line: Keep >450 psi, if lower will kill VANP. Keep High SUC at VANP, if restart VANP: set SUC at 120 psi.
  • COB until grav shows good Diesel = 35-Trainmix @ 50 degree. If open at 1st indication, it takes 20 mins to get to 50 degrees.

EPJT-DEL
EPJT Station Notes:
  • Check the same product in the line fill with both stations.
  • New products -> Diesel or 93CB: Set ticket 1800 bbls (200 go to MIX). 93/87 -> 87: Set ticket 1900 bbls.
  • Open valves base on barrels counts (target bbls at 200 to open valve). This line is very fast so No auto flush. We can open DELM valve early.
  • Carefully check tank line up: SPL is Sunoco (ET); BPL is Buckeye.
  • Pressure change will mess up the gravity -> so open them early. Sometimes gravity jumps to 100 and auto closes the tank.
  • If gravity outside station is 155, open to transmix it may help to reduce the pressure and normalize the gravity.
